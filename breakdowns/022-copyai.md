# Copy.ai Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Copy.ai blog "15 Effective Cold Email Templates for Better Lead Gen", from an AI sales platform
**Template number:** 022
**Emails analyzed:** 0. The title promises 15 templates, but the page contains none, and no example emails. Its only image is an author avatar, so nothing is hidden in images.
**Subject lines:** none
**Performance data:** not provided. The page cites an 8.5% average reply rate and a claim that most sales need five follow-ups. Both are unverified and neither is tied to an email.

> Note on the source: this is a general guide, not a template library. I counted it as a source studied but it adds no emails to any count. Everything below is paraphrase.

## What it does say

- A cold email has five parts: subject, opening line, value proposition, call to action and a follow-up plan.
- Research the audience and ideal customer first, then customize, then track opens, clicks and replies and A/B test.
- Send Tuesday to Thursday, roughly 10am to 2pm, and test it. Plan three or four follow-ups, each adding something.
- Common mistakes: generic emails, no follow-up, weak subject lines.
- Length: 100 to 200 words, which is longer than most other sources here (about 50 to 120).
- It suggests "limited time" and "act now" wording to create urgency, while also warning against being pushy.

## Where it conflicts with the better evidence in this repo

- **Urgency phrases.** "Limited time offer" and "act now" are spam-trigger wording. The checklist keeps manufactured scarcity out.
- **Length.** 100 to 200 words is on the long side. The rest of the studied sources land shorter.
- **No templates.** Nothing here can be quoted or imitated, so no reference tables changed.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The reminder to mention your follow-up plan so the reader expects it.
- Align sales and marketing on one message and one audience.
- Track bounce and unsubscribe rates, not only opens.
