# Outplay Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Outplay blog "10 cold email templates", from a sales engagement platform
**Template number:** 025 (one source, 10 templates analyzed)
**Subject lines:** provided for every template (listed below)
**Performance data:** not provided for any template. The page says fewer than 9% of cold emails get a reply, with no source, so I treat it as unverified.

> Note on the source: a vendor page with text templates only. The page itself warns against pasting templates unchanged. Everything below is paraphrase except subject lines.

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea | Words (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Who it targets and why it fits

Sales teams using sequences. The ten angles cover a first touch, a resource, a trigger and a relationship hook, so reps can pick by situation.

## Psychology used

| Trigger | Where it shows up | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Effort reduction | All 10 | Short |
| Commitment minimization | 8 (a quick call, a question, a recording) | Small asks |
| Specificity | 4 (a report, a milestone, a shared follow, a market change) | Shows research |
| Social proof | 4 (customers, a competitor, a count) | Borrowed credibility |
| Reciprocity | 3 (tips, a resource, a report) | Gives first |
| Quantified benefit | 2 | Concrete gain |
| Topical hook | 2 (research, market news) | Rides a subject they care about |
| Authority, urgency, similarity, competitive comparison, curiosity gap, liking | 1 each | FOMO claim, shared follow, rival result |

## Hook, CTA and personalization

- **Hooks:** a report or trend (2), a milestone, a shared interest, a competitor's result, a user's feedback on their product.
- **CTAs:** a short call (5), a calendar link (2), a question (3), an offer to send a recording (1).
- **Personalization:** the page says to group lists by size or pain and personalize per group, and to test where the right level is.

## Sequence and cadence

No fixed sequence. The page says to always follow up and to move prospects between sequences on behavior, which is a product feature pitch.

## Weaknesses

- **Typos in the source** ("firstnname", an empty line to fill).
- **Vague bodies.** Several templates leave the key sentence as a blank ("The challenge/opportunity this presents is ____").
- **FOMO as a pitch.** The tap-into-FOMO template leans on "world's leading", which is a hard claim to prove.
- **Over-exclamation.** Several subjects use exclamation marks.
- **Long subjects.** A few run past what shows on mobile.
- **Vendor framing and no results.**

## What is genuinely useful here

- Matching the template to the reason for writing (a report, a milestone, a shared follow).
- The point that pasting a template unchanged only gets you unsubscribes.
- Linking a resource back to your product in one line.
