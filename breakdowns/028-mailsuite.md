# Mailsuite Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Mailsuite blog "33 unusual cold email templates to generate more leads", from an email tracking vendor
**Template number:** 028 (one source, 33 templates analyzed)
**Subject lines:** provided for every template (selected ones listed below)
**Performance data:** not provided. The page gives no results for any template.

> Note on the source: a vendor page with short, generic text templates across B2B, e-commerce, hotel, nonprofit, legal, health, school and startup uses. Many are written as mass announcements, not one-to-one outreach, and several use hype words. I treat it as weak evidence and mainly as a source of what to avoid. No images hold emails. Everything below is paraphrase except subject lines.

## Who it targets and why it fits

Mostly small businesses sending announcements to a list. Only a handful (follow-ups, a call setup, the investor teaser) are real one-to-one cold emails.

## Psychology used

| Trigger | Where it shows up | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Effort reduction | All 33 (short) | Quick to read |
| Commitment minimization | About 20 (reply to book, a brief call) | Small asks |
| Reciprocity | About 10 (a free consultation, valuation, demo, sample, review) | Gives first |
| Scarcity and exclusivity | About 5 each ("limited-time", "exclusive") | Time or access pressure |
| Social proof | 4 | Testimonials, a savings story |
| Specificity | 4 (mostly the follow-ups) | Refers to a real meeting |
| Consistency | 3 (the follow-ups) | Continues a conversation |
| Risk reversal | 3 ("no strings attached", "no commitment") | Lowers the stakes |
| Quantified benefit | 2 (a 25% code, a 50% saving) | A concrete gain |
| Authority | 1 ("market leader") | A bare claim |

## Hook, CTA and personalization

- **Hooks:** mostly an announcement of a product, event or offer (about 12), plus a prior meeting or call (3).
- **CTAs:** reply to schedule (6), a short call (8), a link to register or visit (8), a free demo or sample (3), a referral ask (1).
- **Personalization:** a first name and a few brackets. The page tells readers to personalize and not copy verbatim.

## Sequence and cadence

Not given. The page says to follow up and to test, with no timing.

## Weaknesses

- **Hype words** such as "revolutionary", "extraordinary", "prepare to be amazed" and "turbocharged rocket fuel" read as spam.
- **Empty bodies.** Most templates give no specific reason for writing to this person.
- **"Free" and "exclusive" in subjects** raise spam risk and are on the checklist's avoid list.
- **"Once-in-a-lifetime"** in the funding teaser is unsupported hype.
- **Mass announcements**, not tailored emails, and a stray "I hope this email finds you well" opener.
- **No results.**

## What is genuinely useful here

- A recap-and-next-step email after a conversation.
- A free-service offer that names exactly what is free (a valuation, a review).
- "No commitment" stated plainly beside an offer.
- Mostly a reminder of what weak cold emails look like.
