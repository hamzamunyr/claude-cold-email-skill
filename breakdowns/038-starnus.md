# Starnus Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Starnus blog "8 Cold Email Templates That Get Replies", from an AI outbound platform
**Template number:** 038 (one source, 7 emails analyzed, plus one 5-touch sequence plan)
**Subject lines:** provided for every email (listed below)
**Performance data:** not provided. Every number inside the emails (15+ hours a week, 40% better enrichment, 30% of pipeline lost, 25% faster pipeline) is an unsourced placeholder from the vendor, so I treat all of them as unverified.

> Note on the source: a vendor page that ranks its own product as the best fit. The three images are decorative stock photos with a template name on them. Everything below is paraphrase except subject lines (a dash changed to a hyphen).

## The templates

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|

## Who it targets and why it fits

Founders and small sales teams selling to sales leaders. The page groups templates by goal: volume (PAS and one-liner), high-value accounts (value-first and ABM), relationships (mutual connection and sequence), and a soft opener (specific question).

## Psychology used

| Trigger | Where it shows up | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Effort reduction and commitment minimization | All 7 | Short, small asks |
| Specificity | 5 (new hires, a thread, a funding round) | Shows research |
| Quantified benefit | 5 | A number in the pitch |
| Social proof and intent signal | 3 each | Competitors, hiring signals |
| Loss aversion, normalization, liking through compliment | 2 each | "Leaking", "most teams at your scale", congrats |
| Problem agitation, reciprocity, no-ask framing, curiosity gap, borrowed trust, competitive comparison | 1 each | PAS, a free checklist, a shared thread, rivals |

## Hook, CTA and personalization

- **Hooks:** a LinkedIn post or thread (2), team hiring (2), a milestone (2), a competitor switch, a funding round.
- **CTAs:** a short call (3), a question (2), a short breakdown (1), no ask (1).
- **Personalization:** a trigger or signal such as hiring, a post or funding. The page says to personalize on signals, not only the first name.

## Sequence and cadence

Three to five touches over 10 to 14 days across email and LinkedIn. The sample plan runs day 1 email, day 2 LinkedIn note, day 5 LinkedIn comment on their post, day 7 email adding proof, day 10 breakup. Each follow-up should add something, not bump. The page advises 50 to 125 words.

## Weaknesses

- **Invented numbers.** Each template's statistic is a placeholder and must be real before use.
- **Pressure claim.** "3 of your competitors just made this switch" is hard to prove and reads as FOMO.
- **Accusatory subject.** "Your pipeline is leaking" assumes a problem.
- **Weak "mutual connection".** Commenting on the same post is not a real mutual contact.
- **Vendor ranking.** The page ends by promoting its own product.
- **No results per template.**

## What is genuinely useful here

- A "no ask here, just sharing" email built on a real asset.
- A single specific question framed as research.
- A coordinated email and LinkedIn plan where each touch adds something.
- A trigger-based opener tied to a real company event.
