# LeadFuze Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** LeadFuze blog "10 Cold Email Template to Boost Response Rate" (March 2021, updated June 2021), from a lead database vendor
**Template number:** 043 (one source, 10 emails analyzed, plus a list of 23 subject line templates)
**Subject lines:** provided for some emails and as a separate list of 23
**Performance data:** not provided for any template. The page cites that 89% of marketers see email as their main lead channel and a 4,300% return, from other sites that I could not verify.

> Note on the source: an older page whose author critiques each template, calling several of them bad. I record the labels the author gave, since that is the useful part. The images are a hero graphic and GIFs. Everything below is paraphrase except subject lines.

## The 10 emails and the author's verdict

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea | Author's verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General, personalized | not shown | Name, company, a pitch, ask for the right person | "Not that bad", but no opt-out line and asks for an introduction |
| 2 | General, meeting ask | not shown | "A stab in the dark", ask for a 15 minute call on a date or the right person | Bad: generic, multiple CTAs, fixed time |
| 3 | Decision-maker: point me | "Can you point me in the right direction?" | Apologizes, asks who handles a pain point | Popular but does not work without research |
| 4 | Decision-maker: appropriate person | "Appropriate Person?" | Intro, a platform, "while I've got attention", asks who to speak with | Weak: looks like no research |
| 5 | Follow-up: next step | not shown | "I'm not sure what our next step is", asks what makes sense | Good: a one-liner question |
| 6 | Follow-up: noting a follow-up | not shown | "I didn't hear back", asks for a calendar or the right person | Weak: do not say you are following up or that they did not reply |
| 7 | After voicemail: call back | not shown | Left a voicemail, call me back | Weak: unclear if the sender knows them |
| 8 | After voicemail: missed you | "Sorry I missed you" | Why you called, when you will try again, your number | Good follow-up after a voicemail |
| 9 | Breakup: thank you | "Thank You From [Company Name]" | Taking them off follow-ups, assumes interest faded, wishes well | Ineffective: draws negative replies |
| 10 | Breakup: close the file | "Permission to close your file?" | "Closing files", asks permission or the next step | Not believable, though the CTA is good |

## The 23 subject line templates

Examples: "Let's cut to the chase", "You missed it", "Save [number] hours per week with [product/solution]", "[Name], I need your advice", "Where do I even start?", "What would you do with [number] more hours per week?", "Why [company]? And why now?", "[Company] increased sales by [amount] with [product/solution]", "Got problems? We've got solutions!", "I am the walrus!". Several are hype or gimmicks.

## Who it targets and why it fits

General B2B sellers using phone and email. Its main contribution is the verdicts, which agree with several newer sources.

## Psychology used

| Trigger | Where it shows up | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Effort reduction | All 10 | Short |
| Commitment minimization | 8 | Small asks |
| Easy exit | 6 (right-person asks, a permission question) | A no is easy |
| Consistency | 5 (follow-ups, voicemail, breakup) | Continues a thread |
| Forced choice, social proof, self-aware honesty | 1 each | Two options, "similar businesses", "stab in the dark" |

## Hook, CTA and personalization

- **Hooks:** a prior touch (6), a missed call (2), a role or function ask.
- **CTAs:** a right-person ask (6), a 15 minute call, a next-step question (2), a phone number (2), permission to close a file.
- **Personalization:** a name and company. The tips say to research the person and focus on them first, go slowly, use a relevant subject and give value.

## Sequence and cadence

Do not follow up right away. Give people space. A breakup email is a last attempt, but the author says the "thank you" style no longer works and suggests planning a different sequence for the future.

## Weaknesses

- **Dated.** Several templates and stats are from 2021 or earlier.
- **Several are labeled bad by its own author**, which makes the page partly a guide to what not to do.
- **Hype subjects.** The list includes empowerment claims and a nonsense line.
- **No results.**

## What is genuinely useful here

- A one-line follow-up that asks what the next step should be.
- A missed-call email that says why you called and when you will try again.
- The rule not to say "I didn't hear back" or "I'm following up" in a follow-up.
- The advice that a "close your file" line is not believable, and that a breakup email should not read like a legal notice.
