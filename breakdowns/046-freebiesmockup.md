# Freebies Mockup Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Freebies Mockup blog "10 Cold Email Templates That Work Wonders", from a design resources site
**Template number:** 046 (one source, 10 templates analyzed)
**Subject lines:** provided for every template (listed below)
**Performance data:** not provided.

> Note on the source: a general blog post from a design-freebies site, so the templates are generic and not backed by any campaign. The only image is a hero graphic. Everything below is paraphrase except subject lines.

## The 10 templates

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Simple introduction | "Quick Introduction" | "I hope this finds you well", what you do, a quick call |
| 2 | Compliment approach | "Impressed by [Recent Achievement]" | Praise for an article, a shared mission, ideas to share |
| 3 | Value proposition | "Unlock New Potential for [Recipient's Company]" | What you help with, three feature bullets, a call |
| 4 | Storytelling | "A Quick Story About [Relevant Topic]" | A client with a similar challenge and result, a brief call |
| 5 | Question approach | "Quick Question About [Specific Area]" | An open question about challenges, a description of your solution |
| 6 | Data-driven | "Boost Your [Specific Metric] by [x%]" | A statistic, a result for others, a brief chat |
| 7 | Referral | "[Referrer's Name] Suggested I Reach Out" | A referrer, a one-line description, a discussion |
| 8 | Event follow-up | "Great Meeting You at [Event Name]" | A past conversation, a collaboration idea, a call |
| 9 | Seasonal | "Wishing You a Productive [Season/Holiday]" | Best wishes, what you do, a chat about opportunities |
| 10 | Last chance | "Last Chance to Improve [Specific Metric]" | A final note, "spaces are limited", a quick call |

## Psychology used

Effort reduction and commitment minimization appear in all 10 (short bodies, one call ask). Specificity appears in 3 (an article, an event, a detail), social proof and quantified benefit in 2 each, and liking through compliment in 2. Borrowed trust (7), consistency (8), curiosity (5) and scarcity (10) appear once each.

## Hook, CTA and personalization

- **Hooks:** a recent article (1), a referrer, an event, a season, a statistic.
- **CTAs:** a short call (9) and one open question.
- **Personalization:** a name, a company and one detail. The page tells readers to mention the company and a recent event.

## Sequence and cadence

Not given. The only follow-up idea is a "last chance" note.

## Weaknesses

- **Generic bodies.** "I hope this message finds you well" and "unlock new potential" say nothing specific.
- **Fake urgency.** "Last chance" and "spaces are limited" have no real deadline or limit behind them.
- **A greeting email with no reason to write.** The seasonal template is a holiday wish plus a pitch.
- **Hype subjects.** "Unlock New Potential" is a stock phrase.
- **No results.**

## What is genuinely useful here

- A short customer-story email that names a similar client's challenge and result (only with a real client).
- An event follow-up that refers to a specific conversation.
- Mostly useful as examples of what to avoid.
