# Smartlead Cold Email Best Practices Breakdown

**Source:** Smartlead blog "Mastering 15+ Cold Email Best Practices in 2026", from a cold email software vendor
**Type:** practice guide (not a template library). It has one short sample template, so no email count is added.
**Performance data:** the page quotes an average response rate and a "40% positive reply" claim from its own users. I could not verify either, so treat them as vendor claims.

## Writing practices it teaches

- A relevant subject line, short, honest and tied to the body.
- Know the prospect first (role, company, pain) before you write.
- Keep the email short and conversational, one clear message, written about the recipient and not about you.
- Address a real pain point, then offer one small step.
- Proofread. Use visuals sparingly and avoid attachments.
- Include a plain signature in the body, not a graphic.
- Personalize beyond the first name, and follow up with a new angle each time.
- Send at a sensible time and test it, track results and improve.

## Sending practices it teaches (ten "industry insights")

A gap of at least five minutes between sends from one inbox, a do-not-contact list, bounce under 2%, pause weak campaigns, retire weak inboxes, check blacklists, about 50 emails per inbox per day, ramp volume slowly, spintax to vary text, and inbox rotation.

## Weaknesses

- Heavy tool marketing; many practices are "use our software".
- Spintax and rotation are presented as tactics, while other sources here (Martal, citing industry bodies) call rotation and fake engagement risky.
- Unverified rates.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The do-not-contact list and the two-percent bounce line.
- One message per email and "about the recipient, not you".
- Stop a failing campaign early instead of pushing volume.
