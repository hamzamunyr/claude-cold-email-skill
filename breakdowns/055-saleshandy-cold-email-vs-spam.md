# Saleshandy "Cold Email vs Spam" Breakdown

**Source:** Saleshandy blog "Cold Email vs Spam: What's the Difference?" (March 2025, updated 2026), from a cold email software vendor
**Type:** practice guide with 4 sample emails (first email, first follow-up, second follow-up, break-up), so 4 emails are added to the count
**Performance data:** not provided.

## The 4 sample emails

| # | Email | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First email | "{{First Name}}, quick question about {{Company Name}}" | An observation, a common pain with a short agitate, a one-line solution, a similar company result, a 10 minute chat |
| 2 | First follow-up | the same subject | The body is identical to the first email, so it repeats instead of adding anything |
| 3 | Second follow-up | "Should I close your file, {{First Name}}" | "Things get busy", a last check-in, an offer to share how a similar company got a result, no pressure |
| 4 | Break-up | "Closing your file, {{First Name}}" | Says it tried a few times, asks if the problem still matters, offers one idea, steps back |

## Practices extracted

- Verify and categorize the lead list; buy only from a legal, ethical source.
- Technical setup first: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, warm-up, rotation, a dedicated IP (debatable), a working unsubscribe.
- Honest subject line, name the recipient, say why you are reaching out, frame the product as a solution to their need, avoid spam words.
- A recognizable, consistent sender name.
- Give value in follow-ups. 3 to 5 emails per lead over a few weeks, each with a new angle.
- Watch complaints, bounces and replies and adjust.
- Regional laws: CAN-SPAM (accurate sender, identify as an ad, opt-out), GDPR, PECR and CASL need a lawful basis or consent. Not legal advice.

## Weaknesses

- The first follow-up repeats the first email word for word, which contradicts its own advice to add value.
- "Closing your file" and "tried a few times but haven't heard back" are guilt-flavored and are patterns other sources here advise against.
- The pitch for its own tool.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The "should I close your file" check-in as a low-pressure last touch (use it without the guilt line).
- The laws summary as a reminder to check the recipient's region.
