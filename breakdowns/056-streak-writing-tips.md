# Streak "Writing an Effective Cold Email" Breakdown

**Source:** Streak blog "How to write an effective cold email: 20 proven tips and tricks", from a CRM-in-Gmail vendor
**Type:** practice guide (no full sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** not verified; any statistics on the page are secondary.

## The 20 tips in three groups

- **Basic elements:** an optimized subject line, pre-header text, impeccable grammar, and a call to action.
- **The message body:** personalize, keep it short, touch on a pain point, write an effective CTA, be authentic, use humor carefully, use social proof, and do not pitch too hard.
- **Process:** find the right person, use templates, A/B test, send at a good time, avoid spam filters, follow up, stay compliant, and consider AI help.

## Practices extracted

- Pre-header text is a second subject line; write it on purpose so a preview does not show filler.
- Start with the person who will actually care; you may have to begin lower in the company and work up.
- Be authentic: write like a person, and humor works only if it fits the recipient.
- Social proof from a similar company beats a generic claim.
- Do not oversell on the first email; earn the next step.
- Templates are a starting structure, then edit for each person.
- Test one change at a time (subject, send time).

## Weaknesses

- General and list-like; few concrete examples.
- Product promotion for the CRM.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The pre-header reminder and the "do not pitch too hard" check.
- Reaching the right person first, even if that means a more junior contact.
