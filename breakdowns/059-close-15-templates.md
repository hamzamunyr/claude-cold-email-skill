# Close (15 Templates) Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Close blog "15 Best Cold Email Templates That Actually Get Replies" (April 2024, updated 2026), from a sales CRM vendor
**Template number:** 059 (one source, 15 templates analyzed)
**Subject lines:** provided for every template (listed below)
**Performance data:** not provided per template. The page quotes averages (an 8.5% reply rate and 44% open rate) with no source; I treat them as unverified.

> Note on the source: a mix of practice advice and 15 templates. Templates 3 and 4 share one body, and 10 and 11 share one body (both are repeated in the page). Everything below is paraphrase except subject lines.

## The 15 templates

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authentic referral | "[Name], can you help?" | A new platform with early results, "do you handle this? If not, who does?" |
| 2 | Ask for an introduction | "Can you show me the way?" | A similar customer result, ask for the right person |
| 3 | Authority-building referral | "Want [insert metric from case study]?" | Straight to the point, named customers and a metric, 15 minutes tomorrow |
| 4 | Recent event | "Want [insert metric from case study]?" | Same body as 3 with a relevant event added |
| 5 | Quick question | "Quick question" | A number of companies helped, ask for the right person |
| 6 | PAS with a competitor | "Scale [their company name] in [time frame]" | Their tenure, a pain, a named competitor's result |
| 7 | Short and sweet pitch | "Why [your company]?" | How you got their contact, a benefit, a call on a given time |
| 8 | AIDA | "Tired of [pain point]?" | Benefit, three bullets, a call |
| 9 | Manager forward | "FWD [your company] + [their company]" | A forwarded note from an executive asking you to reach out |
| 10 | No-ask outreach | "Your feature on our blog" | You featured them in an article; asks for their thoughts |
| 11 | "My CEO is excited" | "Your feature on our blog" | A near copy of 10 |
| 12 | Can I send you ideas | "Two ideas for [company]" | Relevant customers, an observation, an offer of a short video with two ideas |
| 13 | Making connections | "[Industry] trends and [prospect company name]" | Three trends, customer gains, a quick chat |
| 14 | After an announcement | "[Prospect's Company] and [Your Company]: A Winning Combination?" | Congratulate an award or news, ask about their biggest challenges |
| 15 | Low pressure, data-backed | "[Stat and metric] for [prospect company] in 15 minutes" | A stat in the subject, a 15 minute call |

## Psychology used

Effort reduction in the referral and "who handles this" group (1, 2, 5), commitment minimization in all 15 (a 10 to 15 minute call), social proof in about 11, specificity in about 7, reciprocity in 3 (a feature, a video, a stat), a trigger event in 3, borrowed authority in 2 (the manager forward), and curiosity in 3.

## Hook, CTA and personalization

- **Hooks:** a named result, a trend list, a feature or mention, an award or news event, a competitor's result, a forwarded executive note.
- **CTAs:** right-person asks (4), a 15 minute call (7), an interest check (2), a thoughts or ideas request (3).
- **Personalization:** the page says templates must be edited and tested and calls for research in batches.

## Sequence and cadence

Day 1, day 2 (a different time of day), day 4, day 8 with a possible break-up. It says one clear CTA and no five-link emails.

## Writing practices on the page

Four parts: subject line (relevant, short, personalized, honest urgency), immediate value, one call to action, and a follow-up plan. Aim for 50 to 150 words. Use AI to brainstorm subjects and edit, not to replace the buyer research. Multi-channel outreach and A/B tests are encouraged.

## Weaknesses

- **Duplicate bodies** in templates 3/4 and 10/11, so the true count of distinct templates is 13.
- **The manager forward** (template 9) fakes a forwarded message, which is deceptive and risky.
- **Several bodies are long** with bullets and a call plus a link.
- **Pain subjects** like "Tired of [pain point]?".
- **No results per template.**

## What is genuinely useful here

- The "right person" ask used as the whole email.
- A mention or feature email that asks only for their thoughts.
- A stat or metric in the subject line, if the stat is real.
- Offering a short video with ideas.
- The follow-up spacing with a different send time.
