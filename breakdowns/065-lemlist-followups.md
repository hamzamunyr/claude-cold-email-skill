# lemlist Follow-Up Breakdown

**Source:** lemlist blog "How many cold email follow-ups should you send?" (updated 2026), from an outreach tool vendor
**Source number:** 065 (follow-up source, 0 emails counted)
**Performance data:** The page reports reply-rate data from its own campaigns (a first email at about 4.5% and cumulative reply rates passing 20% at about 10 emails, two example emails with 71% and 33% reply rates). I could not verify them. The two examples are shown as screenshots or partial text, so I do not count them as emails.

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## Cadence and timing

Recommends 4 to 9 follow-ups, with a 2-day gap before email 2, then 4 days, 4 days, and 5 or more days after that, for a total of 10 to 25 days. It says to swap some email follow-ups for LinkedIn and other channel steps rather than adding more touches.

## Weaknesses

- Match made in heaven and similar hype in the sample.
- The data is the vendor's own and counts total sequence replies, which hides the falling reply rate per email.
- Its 4 to 9 range conflicts with the 2 to 3 range in Artisan and Smartlead.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The cumulative view: each follow-up adds fewer replies, but total replies still rise.
- Add value every time, avoid guilt-trip lines like I'm busy too, keep it near 120 words.
- Replace some emails with other channels instead of piling on more.
