# Instantly Follow-Up Breakdown

**Source:** Instantly blog "Cold Email Follow-Up Templates for Every Scenario" (updated May 2026), from a cold email platform vendor
**Source number:** 066 (follow-up source, 16 emails counted)
**Performance data:** It quotes statistics (a 220% reply lift from a first follow-up, 55% of replies coming from follow-ups, a 31% lift from waiting 3 days). I could not verify them.

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold quick bump | "Quick follow-up" | Did this land at a bad time? One peer result |
| 2 | Cold case study | "[Company Name] + [Their Industry] results" | A quick win, metric, setup time, 15 minutes |
| 3 | Cold breakup | "Closing the loop" | Assume it is not a priority, name a future quarter to revisit |
| 4 to 7 | Post-meeting set | "Great meeting today - next steps", "Resources to share with your team", "Checking in - [Project Name]", "Let's reschedule" | Recap with owners and dates, stakeholder materials, a one-week check-in, missed-meeting rebook |
| 8 to 12 | Proposal set | "Re: [Project Name] proposal", "...timeline", "...ROI breakdown", "...closing the loop" | Any questions, a timeline check, a gentle nudge, an ROI reminder, a final breakup |
| 13 to 16 | Objection set | "Re: Pricing for [Project Name]", "Re: Timing for [Project Name]", "Re: [Current Tool] comparison", "Should I close your file?" | Price, timing, comparison and a close-the-file email |

## Cadence and timing

Cold sequence: follow-up 1 on day 3 (add one new value), follow-up 2 on day 7 (social proof), follow-up 3 on day 12 (breakup). Gaps of 2 to 5 days, most at 3. For demos send the recap within 24 hours; for proposals follow up in 2 days, then every 3 to 4 days for 4 to 5 touches over 3 to 4 weeks, then a breakup.

## Weaknesses

- Many templates are for later deal stages, not cold outreach.
- Quote Did this land at a bad time? is a soft guilt check.
- The page is long and promotes its own platform.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The recap with named owners and dates, and the stakeholder-enablement email.
- Reply in the proposal thread to keep context.
- A 3-step cold sequence: add value, then proof, then close the loop.
