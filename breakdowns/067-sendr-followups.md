# Sendr Follow-Up Breakdown

**Source:** Sendr blog "10 Cold Email Follow-Up Templates That Actually Get Responses", from an AI outreach platform
**Source number:** 067 (follow-up source, 10 emails counted)
**Performance data:** Not provided. It states community numbers (70% of opportunities after the third touch, 4 to 7 total emails) with no source.

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gentle reminder plus new value | "Re: [Original Subject]" | Circle back, share an article on how companies in their industry handle a problem |
| 2 | Pattern-interrupt video | "A quick video for you, {{firstName}}" | A 20-second personal video, ask if it fits their goals |
| 3 | Trigger event | "Congrats on the [Trigger Event]!" | Congratulate, name the new priority the event creates |
| 4 | Question only | "Quick question" | Are you the right person? If not, who is? |
| 5 | Social proof | "Idea for {{company_name}}" | A similar company's result and a case study link |
| 6 | Respectful bump | "Re: [Original Subject]" | Bring the thread back to the top, any thoughts? |
| 7 | Breakup | "Closing your file" | I will take that to mean it is not a priority, I won't follow up again |
| 8 | Insight drop | "A stat you might find interesting" | One real statistic and why it matters |
| 9 | Re-engagement | "Popping back up" | What you discussed months ago, what changed, a 15-minute catch-up |
| 10 | Dynamic background video | "Your website + an idea" | A short video showing their site, ask if it fits their goals |

## Cadence and timing

Timing: first follow-up 2 to 3 business days later, second 3 to 4 business days after that, then 5 to 7 business days. Three phases: close together first, wider spacing in the middle, a breakup at the end. Suggests mixing a data email, a question email and a video email.

## Weaknesses

- Template 6 is the bump pattern other sources say is weak; the page itself says to use it only late.
- Videos that fake a personal recording with AI voice or lip sync can feel deceptive to a recipient.
- Template 7 says haven't heard back and assumes meaning from silence.
- A vendor pitch under each template.

## What is genuinely useful here

- A sequence that mixes types (data, question, video) so it never repeats.
- The are you the right person? question as the cheapest ask.
- The 90-day re-engagement tied to what they said before.
