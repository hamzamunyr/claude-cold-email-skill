# Ashley Gainer Follow-Up Breakdown

**Source:** Ashley Gainer blog and podcast "How to Follow Up with Cold Emails" (2020), from a freelance writing coach
**Source number:** 069 (follow-up source, 0 emails counted)
**Performance data:** Not provided.

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## Cadence and timing

Follow up at least twice, ideally three times, then stop. Wait one week for the first follow-up, another week for the second, one more week for the third. Respect any deadline named in a job post. If someone says to come back later, confirm and set a reminder.

## Weaknesses

- Written for freelancers pitching writing work, so some advice does not carry to B2B sales.
- It says some people follow up ten times, which she rejects.
- Only short example phrases, not full emails.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Tell them when you will be back in touch, then do it.
- Say clearly when it is the last email.
- A very short are you still looking for X? email when they came to you first.
