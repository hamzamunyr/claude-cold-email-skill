# Mixmax Follow-Up Breakdown

**Source:** Mixmax blog "Cold Email Follow-Up Sequence: Get Replies" (April 2026), from a sales engagement tool vendor
**Source number:** 070 (follow-up source, 4 emails counted)
**Performance data:** Cites a CRM study that 4 to 7 follow-ups get 3 times the replies of 1 to 3 and that only 8% of reps follow up more than five times. Unverified.

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Helpful resource | new thread subject with a hint of value | Share one relevant resource to re-engage a quiet prospect |
| 2 | Social proof | not shown in the text I could read | A comparable customer result |
| 3 | Quick question | "Re: [Original Subject Line]" | Is improving [pain point] a priority for you right now? |
| 4 | Breakup | not shown in the text I could read | A polite last email |

## Cadence and timing

The 2-4-7-14 cadence: first follow-up after 2 days, second 4 days later, third 7 days after that, fourth 14 days after that. Increase the gap each time. Vary the time of day. Do not follow up the very next day.

## Weaknesses

- Parts of the page are generic tips.
- Only template 3 was fully readable in the text version I got.
- Tool promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The 2-4-7-14 cadence with widening gaps.
- Specific CTA: suggest a time (Tuesday at 10?) not let me know your thoughts.
- Ask for a single yes or no on whether the pain is a priority.
