# Coldreach Follow-Up Breakdown

**Source:** Coldreach blog "Cold Email Follow-Up: Timing, Templates, and Sequences That Get Replies" (June 2026), from an AI outreach vendor
**Source number:** 071 (follow-up source, 8 emails counted)
**Performance data:** Not provided for the templates (the page header shows a 3.8% reply claim for its own product, unverified).

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research-based | "Re: [original subject]" | Following up because of a specific company observation, the pain it creates, is this something your team is dealing with, or already solved? |
| 2 | Wrong person? | "Re: [original subject]" | Who owns the problem, why I reached out, no worries if not relevant |
| 3 | Pain hypothesis | "Re: [original subject]" | I may be off, but my guess is... with the usual workaround and where it breaks, is that accurate? |
| 4 | Proof point | "Re: [original subject]" | A similar team's pain and outcome, admit it may not map, worth a quick conversation? |
| 5 | Useful resource | "Re: [original subject]" | A resource even if now is not the time, want me to send it over? |
| 6 | Direct qualification | "Re: [original subject]" | Are you trying to improve [metric], or is that not a priority? |
| 7 | Breakup | "close the loop?" | I will close the loop, why I reached out, no need to reply if not a priority |
| 8 | No-response example | not given | A concrete question about how their reps do research, with the reason for asking |

## Cadence and timing

A 3 to 5 email sequence: Day 1, Day 3 or 4, Day 7 or 8, Day 12 to 15, Day 20 to 25. Each email has a different job: pain, trigger, proof, qualification question, close the loop. It says to avoid saying saw you opened my email.

## Weaknesses

- Templates are skeletons; they need real research to work.
- Almost all use the Re: subject, which should be a real reply in an existing thread, never fake.
- Vendor pitch for its AI.

## What is genuinely useful here

- One new piece of context per follow-up; never repeat the same ask.
- The pain hypothesis email that invites correction.
- A direct qualification question so you stop guessing.
- Do not mention opens.
