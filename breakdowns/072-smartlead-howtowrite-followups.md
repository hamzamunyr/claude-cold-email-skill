# Smartlead 25 Follow-Up Templates Breakdown

**Source:** Smartlead blog "How to Write a Follow Up Email [Complete Guide + 25 Proven Templates]", from a cold email software vendor
**Source number:** 072 (follow-up source, 25 emails counted)
**Performance data:** Not provided. The guide gives general advice and no measured results for the templates.

> Note on the source: this is a follow-up guide. Only the subject lines are quoted; everything else is paraphrase.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | No response sequence | "Checking in on my last email", "Idea for [Prospect's Company]", "[Name], let's try another approach", "Closing the loop" | Gentle nudge, a resource, a different angle with a call, breakup |
| 5 to 6 | After a meeting | "Great talking with you - next steps", "Following up on our meeting - more info" | Recap with a next step, extra information |
| 7 to 8 | After a proposal | "Thoughts on the proposal?", "Additional info for your proposal" | Ask for thoughts, pre-empt an objection |
| 9 to 11 | Event, voicemail, referral | "Great to meet you at [Event]!", "Sorry I missed you earlier", "Following up on [Referrer's Name]'s introduction" | Reference the shared moment |
| 12 to 15 | More cold and re-engagement | "[Name], see how we helped [Similar Company]", "Quick win idea for you", "It's been a while...", "Should I keep the proposal open?" | Case study, a small win with no strings, a long-time-no-talk check, a proposal breakup |
| 16 to 18 | Job and networking | "Following up on my application for [Position]", "Thank you for the interview yesterday", "Great to connect at [Event]" | Not cold sales, applies to careers and networking |
| 19 to 25 | Account stages | "Questions after our demo?", "How's your trial going? Need any help?", "Upcoming renewal - any questions?", "Checking in - how are things at [Company]?", "Reviewing your case study draft", "Following up from [Networking Event]", "Closing out our offer (ends [Day])" | Demo, trial, renewal, stay in touch, testimonial, event, last-chance offer |

## Cadence and timing

Wait 2 to 3 days before the first follow-up and avoid generic just checking in subjects. Subjects of 3 to 7 words, test one change at a time.

## Weaknesses

- The first template is the checking in style the same page warns against.
- Only about 6 of the 25 are cold-email follow-ups; the rest are job, account and event emails.
- Template 25 depends on a real deadline.
- Vendor pitch.

## What is genuinely useful here

- A small-win email: share one thing they can do now, no strings attached.
- Different angle follow-up (a new idea instead of repeating the first).
- A subject line quality table comparing good and bad examples.
