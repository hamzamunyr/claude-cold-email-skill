# Omentir Good Reply Rate Breakdown

**Source:** Omentir help article "What is a good cold email reply rate?", from an AI outreach vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Overall reply rates in 2026 vendor data run about 3 to 8%; positive replies about 1 to 3% of delivered emails; healthy open rates are quoted as 40 to 60% but are a weaker number now.
- A well-targeted campaign should aim for 5 to 10%; 10% and up usually needs a small, sharp segment, not a 20,000-row blast.

## Practices extracted

- Calculate reply rate as replies divided by delivered emails, not sent and not opened.
- If you get 0% after a couple of hundred delivered emails, check deliverability, list problems, spam placement, bounces and domain age before rewriting copy.
- Compare campaigns inside your own account rather than against a blog.
- Track out-of-office replies separately, honor unsubscribes fast, and warm new domains (often 2 to 4 weeks) before judging copy.
- Use LinkedIn when identity matters more than volume.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The "compare to yourself, not a blog" rule and the check order for a zero-reply campaign.
