# Sera Manufacturer Reply Benchmarks Breakdown

**Source:** Sera knowledge base "B2B cold email reply rate benchmarks for manufacturers", from a lead generation vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Average about 3.43%; broad campaigns 1 to 4%; highly targeted industrial outreach 5 to 10% and 15% or more for the best; over 1,000 recipients about 2.1%; 50 or fewer about 5.8%.
- Claims personalized industrial emails get 29% higher opens and 202% higher replies, and that 17% of cold emails miss the inbox because of authentication problems.

## Practices extracted

- Precision over volume; configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
- Personalize with something concrete to the buyer (a facility location, a production milestone, a shared supply chain challenge).
- Cadence of 8 to 12 touchpoints over four weeks with the first follow-up after 3 to 5 days (this is far longer than most sources here).

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The idea of personalizing on an operational detail (a site, a milestone) for industrial buyers.
