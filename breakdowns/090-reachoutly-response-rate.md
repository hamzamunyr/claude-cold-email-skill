# Reachoutly Response Rate Guide Breakdown

**Source:** Reachoutly blog "Cold Email Response Rate (2026 Guide)" (updated May 2026), from an outreach agency. This is a different page from the earlier Reachoutly template source.
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Average 3.43% and above 5% good; 3 to 5% typical entering 2026; its own 2025 campaigns averaged about 7%; positive replies 0.5 to 2%; meeting booking rate about 2.3% in Q1 2026.
- By campaign size: up to 50 recipients 5.8%, 51 to 250 4.6%, 251 to 1,000 3.2%, over 1,000 2.1%. By personalization: none 1.5%, name and company 2.8%, role pain point 4.2%, trigger-based 6.1% or more.
- Industry ranges (legal 6 to 10%, recruiting 5 to 8%, software 2 to 4%, financial 1.5 to 3.5%) and country figures that vary widely (from about 1% to about 17%).
- Verified lists reply about twice as well; B2B contact data decays about 22.5% a year.

## Practices extracted

- Count only unique human replies from delivered emails, excluding out-of-office and automated messages; report positive and negative rates beside the total.
- Re-verify contacts every 90 days; keep campaigns small and tightly segmented.
- Plain text for cold outreach; 50 to 125 words (under 80 for a first touch); a clear low-friction call to action; subject lines under 50 characters tied to a problem or trigger.
- Dedicated cold domain, bounces under 2%, complaints under 0.1%, warm up 2 to 4 weeks starting at 5 to 10 a day; sequences of 5 to 7 emails spaced 3 to 5 days apart.
- Feed AI specific inputs (funding, hiring, launches, role pain) and edit every output for natural voice.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The reply-counting rules (unique human replies, excluding automated ones) and the contact data decay reminder.
