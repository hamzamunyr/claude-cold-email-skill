# Salesmate Response Rate Guide Breakdown

**Source:** Salesmate blog "What is the Average Cold Email Response Rate?", from a CRM vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Average 1 to 5% (B2B about 3 to 5%); it also quotes a 7% average attributed to an agency study, a client campaign over 38% and a startup campaign at 20%. LinkedIn InMail response is quoted at 18 to 25%.

## Practices extracted

- Segment the list around one persona and validate emails.
- Write a custom intro of 1 to 3 lines about something specific to the prospect.
- Follow up within 2 days and automate the sequence; test mid-week mornings.
- A rule of thumb: expect replies around 30 to 50% of your open rate (unreliable because open rates are inflated).

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The one to three line custom intro rule. The 38% client figure is an outlier I do not rely on.
