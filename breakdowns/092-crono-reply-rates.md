# Crono Reply Rates Playbook Breakdown

**Source:** Crono Academy "From 1 to 8.5%: Cold Email Reply Rates Benchmarks & B2B Playbook" (September 2026), from a sales automation vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Baseline 1 to 5% for broad or lightly personalized campaigns; 8.5% and up for highly targeted lists (a practitioner post is the cited source); a 12 to 18% reply lift is claimed for 4 to 7 word subject lines.

## Practices extracted

- Measure any reply, positive reply and meeting booked as three separate numbers, from delivered emails, cohorted by list source, campaign and persona.
- Wait for 100 to 200 delivered emails per variant before trusting a comparison; change one variable at a time.
- Fix deliverability and list quality first; subject lines of 4 to 7 words; micro-personalization that scales (a funding round, a job change, a pain point); a small first ask.
- 3 to 5 touch sequence a few days apart, each adding something new, with LinkedIn or calls between emails.
- Shift value and shrink the ask as the sequence goes on: an opener, a follow-up with new information or a case reference, then a yes or no ask, then a breakup.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Shrinking the ask across the sequence, and counting any reply, positive reply and meeting as separate numbers.
