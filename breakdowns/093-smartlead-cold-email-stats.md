# Smartlead 27 Cold Email Stats Breakdown

**Source:** Smartlead blog "27 Cold Email Statistics You Need to Know in 2025", from a cold email software vendor. This is a different page from the earlier Smartlead sources.
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- A grab bag of re-quoted figures: 8.5% of cold emails get responses; multi-point personalization lifts replies by 142%; sending to several people at one company is quoted as raising replies 93%; 80% of sales take 5 or more follow-ups; unsubscribe about 2.2%; bounce about 7.5%.
- Gong-attributed phrase figures: saying never heard back lowers meetings by 14%, and an opinion-style ask lowers meetings by 20% while interest-based asks do far better; longer emails of about 4 or more sentences are quoted as performing about 15 times better. Other claims (hope this finds you well raising meetings 24%) conflict with advice elsewhere.
- Subject claims: numbers lift opens 113%, questions 21%, a company name 22%, first-name subjects 43% reply (clearly inconsistent with the other benchmarks).

## Practices extracted

- Personalize subjects and body, target a pain point and a defined customer profile, use interest-based calls to action, keep the list clean.
- Send Monday or Tuesday 1 to 3 pm (a claim from a different dataset than the morning advice elsewhere).

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The warning that interest-based asks beat opinion-style asks and that never heard back hurts. Its sample subject lines are generic ("Quick question", "Collaboration with [company]") or hype-style, and some use emojis, which I do not recommend.
