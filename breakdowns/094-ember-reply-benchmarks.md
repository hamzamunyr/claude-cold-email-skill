# Ember Reply Benchmarks Breakdown

**Source:** Ember research article "Cold Email Reply Rate Benchmarks and Practical Metrics" (October 2026), from a lead intelligence vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- It repeats the first-party Belkins study: about 0.45% average reply across 7.5 million emails, founders and owners 0.57% vs C-level 0.42% vs VPs 0.32%, companies of 0 to 10 employees 0.72% vs 10,000 or more 0.22%, mornings (8 to noon) best, US about 0.51%.
- It also quotes Gong figures: the average rep sends about 344 cold emails to get one meeting; top reps book 8.1 times more meetings; pitching the product in the first email cuts replies by up to 57%; buzzwords or numbers in subjects can lower opens.

## Practices extracted

- Calculate against total sends leaving your server or against delivered, state which one, and avoid mixing definitions.
- Do not apply the same volume to enterprise accounts (low baseline); send 8 to noon; keep emails to about 100 words or fewer in three or four sentences; do not pitch the product in the first email.
- Combine email with professional networks; keep a clear opt-out and legitimate-interest process under GDPR.
- Keep a human in the loop for review of signal-based drafts.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Confirms the Belkins first-party numbers and adds the do-not-pitch-in-email-one advice.
