# SalesCaptain Response Rates Breakdown

**Source:** SalesCaptain blog "Cold Email Response Rates Explained: Benchmarks, Tactics, and How to Improve", from an agency. This is a different page from the earlier SalesCaptain follow-up source.
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary. I could not verify any figure, so every number below is an unverified vendor claim.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Average 1 to 5%; well-optimized 8 to 12%; founder-to-founder with a clear value exchange 20% or more; a mass-blasted scraped list is lucky to reach 0.5%.

## Practices extracted

- Role accuracy, industry relevance and firmographic fit in the list; segment by title, tech stack, growth stage, hiring signals, size, geography and pain patterns.
- Personalize by a recent event, a shared community or podcast, a tech stack or a job responsibility.
- 50 to 125 words, one specific or binary ask (for example a 10 minute call next week or is this on your radar?); avoid let me know if you want more info.
- 3 to 4 touch sequence across 2 to 3 weeks, 3 to 4 days apart; A/B test one variable; track pipeline conversion as well as reply intent.
- Subject lines that are clear and relevant, not clickbait. Its examples ("Quick question about [company]") are generic.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Two good personalization examples (a first RevOps hire, a podcast episode) and the specific-or-binary ask guidance.
