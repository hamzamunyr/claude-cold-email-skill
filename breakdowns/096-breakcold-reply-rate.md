# Breakcold Reply Rate Guide Breakdown

**Source:** Breakcold blog "Cold Email Reply Rate (Ultimate Guide 2026)", from a sales CRM vendor. This is a different page from the earlier Breakcold sources.
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no full sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify any figure. Several claims are outliers (a "good" rate above 15%, top senders at 40 to 50%, LinkedIn reply rates around 60%), which conflict with the first-party data in the Belkins breakdown (#086) and with most other sources, so I treat them as unreliable.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- A good reply rate above 15%, average 5 to 12%, bad below 5%, mass cold email about 2%, and top senders 40 to 50%. LinkedIn is said to reply far more than email.
- Wait 7 to 10 days before judging a campaign's final reply rate.

## Practices extracted

- Judge by reply rate, not opens (opens measure deliverability).
- Use a one or two sentence pitch, under about 100 words, and avoid sounding pushy.
- Separate positive and negative replies to check you are targeting the right people.
- Test different buyer personas to find the right one.
- When you lack time for full personalization, segment by location or industry and write a semi-personalized intro.
- Run email and LinkedIn from one pipeline.
- Do not be too casual, since prospects are not friends yet.
- The page also suggests using memes or GIFs in follow-ups for humor; other sources here advise against gimmicks, so I do not adopt it.

## Weaknesses

- Outlier benchmarks and a vendor pitch.
- The sample pitch line is a product claim (hours saved, a 50% faster sales cycle) with no support.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The 7 to 10 day wait before reading a campaign's final reply rate.
- Testing different buyer personas, and the semi-personalized intro fallback.
