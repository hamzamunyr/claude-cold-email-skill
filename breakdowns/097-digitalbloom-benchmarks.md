# Digital Bloom Reply Benchmarks Breakdown

**Source:** The Digital Bloom "Cold Email Reply-Rate Benchmarks" (published 2025, updated 2026), from a marketing agency
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Quotes an analysis of about 11 million emails (a data-vendor study) that personalization depth lifts replies about 52% and that small, targeted campaigns beat broad blasts by about 2.8 times.
- Headline claim: timeline-based asks (for example, worth 15 minutes this week) earn about 2.3 times the reply rate and 3.4 times the meetings of problem-based hooks.

## Practices extracted

- Use timeline hooks when emailing C-suite buyers; segment by ICP and industry, not only size; keep cohorts under about 50 contacts.
- A 3-7-7 cadence (day 0, 3, 10, 17) and stop after 2 follow-ups (3 touches max).
- Prefer deep personalization to send-time optimization; send 10 to 11 am or 2 to 4 pm local on Tuesday to Thursday; judge by positive reply and meeting rate.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The timeline-ask idea (a specific, bounded time ask) and the stop-after-three-touches rule.
