# ReplyLead Realistic Reply Rate Breakdown

**Source:** ReplyLead "What Is a Realistic Cold Email Reply Rate?" (2026), from an outreach agency
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** This page reports first-party campaign data (81 campaigns with at least 500 contacted leads, 115 campaigns in a wider pool). It is small and unaudited, but it is unusually careful about definitions. I read it through an extraction summary.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Median reply rate per contacted lead 2.12% (quartiles 1.38% and 2.97%, 90th percentile 3.79%, max 8.00%); unweighted mean 2.38%.
- Pooled across 115 campaigns: 2.58% replies per contact, 1.45% replies per send (about 1.8 sends per contact), bounce 1.32%.
- It compares this with a 3.43% overall average quoted from another vendor and says its own number is descriptive, not a forecast.

## Practices extracted

- Check the definition (per contact, per send, per delivered; mean, median or pooled) before comparing a headline to your own results.
- Use your own stage records and opt-out records to judge lead quality and whether to continue a sequence.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- A candid small-sample median of about 2% that matches the first-party Belkins finding that real campaigns average far below blog benchmarks, and the call to check definitions.
