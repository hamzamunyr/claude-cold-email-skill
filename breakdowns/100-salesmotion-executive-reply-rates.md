# Salesmotion Executive Email Data Breakdown

**Source:** Salesmotion blog "Cold Emailing Executives: What the Reply Data Shows" (2026), from a sales signal vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** It cites a conversation intelligence vendor for the executive reply gap and reports its own small test. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- C-level executives are said to be about 30% less likely to reply than non-executives; they decide whether to open in about three seconds and read for at most nine.
- In its own list-based campaigns the human reply rate was about 0.3% across 12,382 leads, with automatic replies outnumbering human ones about 14 to 1.

## Practices extracted

- Subject lines of one to four words about their world, not what you sell; body under 100 words (sweet spot 50 to 100).
- Lead with their priorities and peer proof, not features; offer value (a benchmark, research, a peer comparison) instead of a meeting.
- Follow up 2 to 3 times, 3 to 5 days apart, each with new value; add a LinkedIn touch after email one and a call after email two; personalize the first two lines and the ask, template the middle by persona.
- Measure human and positive replies only.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- A five-line executive structure: a specific event or initiative, the challenge it creates, how others handle it, a peer-style question, and a low-pressure close. Example subject lines it gives are specific nouns such as a risk or forecast topic.
