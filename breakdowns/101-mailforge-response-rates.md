# Mailforge Response Rate Guide Breakdown

**Source:** Mailforge blog "Average Cold Email Response Rates 2026", from an email infrastructure vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims. Several figures conflict with other pages, which suggests copying errors.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- A grab bag: range 1 to 8.5%; 5.8% for 50 or fewer recipients vs 2.1% over 1,000; contacting 2 to 4 people at one company reaches 7.8% vs 2.5% for 5 to 10 people (this is the opposite of an earlier Backlinko-attributed claim on another page); executives reply about 23% more than non-executives (the opposite of the Salesmotion figure); first follow-up lifts replies about 49%, the third drops them.
- Industry ranges: legal about 10%, education technology about 7.8%, financial about 3.4%, software about 1 to 3.5%.

## Practices extracted

- Relevance and personalization over volume; 50 to 125 words; subject lines under 50 characters, no spam words; 2 to 3 follow-ups starting at day 3; contact 2 to 4 people per company; plain technical setup, warm-up and list cleaning.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The reminder that these pages contradict each other (executive reply rate, contacts per company), which is why the skill benchmarks against your own results.
