# Cleanlist Response Statistics Breakdown

**Source:** Cleanlist blog "Cold Email Response Rate Statistics" (February 2026, updated July 2026), from a contact data vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims. Its figures are labeled as aggregated from other vendors.

## Figures it states (unverified)

- Average reply 3.1%, open about 42%, bounce 5.1%, positive reply 1.4%, meeting 0.7%, unsubscribe 2.1%.
- Verified vs unverified vs purchased lists: reply about 4.6%, 2.3% and 0.8%; bounce about 1.2%, 7.8% and 18.5%; complaints about 0.1%, 0.4% and 1.8%.
- Sequence steps: email 1 about 2.2%, email 2 about 1.8%, email 3 about 1.4%, email 4 and later about 0.6% (cumulative about 6%).
- Reply by vertical: recruiting about 5.8 to 7.2%, agency to small business about 4.2%, software about 2.4%, enterprise software about 1.8%, financial about 1.5%. B2B contact data decays about 22.5% a year.

## Practices extracted

- Verify every address before it enters a sequence; re-enrich contacts older than 90 days; remove purchased lists.
- Authenticate, use a separate cold domain, warm 2 to 4 weeks, cap 30 to 50 per mailbox per day; under 100 words, one clear question, a 3 to 5 email sequence over 14 to 21 days.
- Weekly targets: bounce under 2%, reply 3% or more, positive reply 1.5% or more, complaints under 0.1%.

## Weaknesses

- Re-quoted or self-reported benchmarks with no clear method, and some conflict with other sources here.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The list-quality comparison table and the per-step reply curve showing diminishing returns.
