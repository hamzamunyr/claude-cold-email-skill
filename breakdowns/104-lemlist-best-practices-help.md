## Practices extracted

- Subject lines of 3 to 5 words; avoid spam triggers and fake urgency; personalize when you can.
- Under 120 words (ideal 80 to 100); 2 to 3 short paragraphs; the first line about them with a specific trigger, observation or hypothesis; value and outcomes, not features; one low-commitment call to action.
- Send Tuesday to Thursday, 8 to 10 am or 3 to 5 pm in their time zone, from a personal address using your real name, in plain text with no attachment in the first email.
- Cadence of 7 to 8 touches (2 to 3 days, then 3 to 4, then 5 to 7, then weekly) that changes the angle, adds new information and uses a P.S.; this is far longer than the skill default.
- Test in this order: subject line, first line, length, call to action type, send time; one variable at a time; at least about 100 sends per variant.
- Mistakes: talking about yourself, jargon, product-first copy, too formal or too casual, no proofreading, one email for everyone.
