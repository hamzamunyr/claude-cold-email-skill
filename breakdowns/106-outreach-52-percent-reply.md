# Outreach 52 Percent Reply Rate Breakdown

**Source:** Outreach blog "How to get a 52-percent reply rate on cold emails" (2017, last modified 2026), from a sales engagement vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no full sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** The headline is one salesperson's result of a 52% response rate on 340 emails sent with the vendor's tool, reported secondhand with no method or list details. The body then calls the section "5 steps to a 52-percent open rate", so the page itself mixes up replies and opens. Treat the 52% as an anecdote, not a benchmark.

## Figures and claims

- One salesperson reported a 52% response on 340 emails (anecdote), with no method, list or offer details.

## Practices extracted

- Find the right prospect: use revenue, headcount, technology used, funding and business type, and niche down as far as possible. Time the campaign off triggers: a new trial of a competitor product, new leadership, or companies using a complementary product.
- Create an authentic campaign: send a short personal LinkedIn connection note, mention it in the first email the next day, never pitch in the very first email, and end with a recommendation. Self-test and friend-test the email (would you reply, does it sound like a person), then templatize by persona and keep testing.
- Cadence: no fewer than 3 and no more than 7 touches; always follow up after the first email; follow up 3 to 4 times; mix phone calls and email or LinkedIn; send when prospects are most active; space the touches.
- Test: include at least one A/B test per sequence and look at what they open, reply to and opt out of.

## Weaknesses

- An old page (2017) with tool references that are outdated; the 52% figure is unverifiable and the replies versus opens confusion undermines it.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The self-test and friend-test before sending, and the idea of not pitching in the first email.
