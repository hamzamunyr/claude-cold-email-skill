# Tinkogroup Response Rates Breakdown

**Source:** Tinkogroup blog "Average Cold Email Response Rates" (2024, updated 2026), from a marketing agency
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no full sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** It re-quotes figures with conflicting ranges (a typical 8.5%, "reasonable" 20 to 30%, top quartile 20% or more) and no method. I could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims. I read the page through an extraction summary.

## Figures and claims

- Typical response about 8.5%; about half of campaigns below 10% reply, a quarter 10 to 20%, a quarter above 20% (a quoted outside distribution); spacing sends over four weeks said to lift responses 20%.

## Practices extracted

- Relevance and personalization beyond the first name; a concise subject line and one clear call to action; a recognizable sender; keep lists clean; follow CAN-SPAM (accurate sender, opt-out, labeling); keep copy short with short paragraphs; proofread; test subject, content, call to action and timing; respond to replies within 24 hours.

## Weaknesses

- Generic advice and figures that clash with other sources; a quoted 20 to 30% "reasonable" rate is not credible next to first-party data.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Little new beyond a reminder of the CAN-SPAM basics and answering replies within a day.
