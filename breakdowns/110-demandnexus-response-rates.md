# Demand Nexus Response Rates Breakdown

**Source:** Demand Nexus "Cold Email Response Rates: Benchmarks & How to Improve" (2026), from a lead qualification agency
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no full sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** It cites its own site sections for the figures and sells qualification services. I could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims. I read the page through an extraction summary.

## Figures and claims

- Average 1 to 5%; single-channel B2B email 2 to 3%; top campaigns 15 to 25%; open rates 20 to 40%; conversion to booked meetings 0.3 to 1%; multichannel sequences over 14 days 18 to 25% engagement.
- By role: C-level 6.4%, VP 5.5%, director 5.2%, manager 3.5% (this conflicts with other pages saying executives reply least). By industry: professional services 6 to 8%, SaaS 3 to 5%, financial 2 to 3%, healthcare 2 to 4%.

## Practices extracted

- Measure outcomes (qualified pipeline), not only responses; spend about 10 to 15 minutes researching each prospect; use multichannel sequences over 14 days.
- Reference specific context (a webinar, a funding round, a pain point); 50 to 125 words with one question; 4 to 7 touches 3 to 5 days apart; qualify (budget, authority, need, timeline) before booking a meeting; send early morning or mid-afternoon local time.

## Weaknesses

- Agency pitch for qualification; executive reply figures contradict other sources.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The reminder to qualify a reply before booking a meeting, and to measure pipeline outcomes.
