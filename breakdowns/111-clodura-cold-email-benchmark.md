# Clodura Cold Email Benchmark Breakdown

**Source:** Clodura blog "Cold Email Benchmark 2026" (June 2026), from a sales data vendor
**Type:** benchmark and practice guide (no full sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** It aggregates figures from other vendors without a method. I could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims. I read the page through an extraction summary.

## Figures and claims

- Open about 30 to 35%, reply about 3 to 3.5%, positive reply about 0.5 to 1% (1 to 2% good), bounce 3 to 5% (under 2% strong), complaints under 0.1%, unsubscribe under 1%, and a meeting booking rate of 10 to 15% of positive replies (an unusual definition).

## Practices extracted

- Cap sends at 50 to 100 a day per domain when starting (up to 200 for well-warmed domains); verify every address; 4 to 6 step sequences, 2 to 4 days apart (5 to 7 for senior buyers); track open, reply, positive reply, bounce, booking and completion rates.
- SPF, DKIM and DMARC on every domain; warm up 4 to 6 weeks; mid-week sends at 8 to 10 am or 3 to 4 pm local; subject lines of 6 words or fewer; 50 to 125 words.

## Weaknesses

- Aggregated figures, some at odds with first-party data; volume caps are higher than the skill default.
- Vendor promotion.

## What is genuinely useful here

- A compact metric glossary with healthy ranges.
