# Smartlead Perfect Cold Email Structure Breakdown

**Source:** Smartlead blog "Effective Cold Email Structure for High Open and Reply Rates", from a cold email software vendor. A different page from the earlier Smartlead sources.
**Type:** structure guide with 5 sample emails (5 added to the count)
**Performance data:** Not provided for the emails. It quotes a personalized-subject open-rate lift and that 80% of sales follow the fifth follow-up, both secondhand. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PAS | "Elevate Your Team's Productivity" | A rhetorical pain question, an escalation, then the product as the fix, let's chat |
| 2 | AIDA | "Optimize Your Workflow with [Your Solution]" | A question, imagine, picture a future, a quick call next week |
| 3 | Quick question | "Quick Question About Your Marketing Strategy" | Ask their go-to strategy, a why this matters section and next steps with a call option |
| 4 | BAB | "Streamline Your Team's Workflow" | Scattered tasks, a picture of an organized workflow, the product, a brief call |
| 5 | Praise, picture, push | "Your Achievements Inspired Us!" | Praise a remarkable achievement, picture a bigger outcome, ask for a call to explore synergy |

## Cadence and timing

Three checkpoints: get noticed (subject), explain intent (body), show value (call to action). Subject lines of roughly 6 to 10 words with a personal touch and value up front; front-load the message; short sentences; value-oriented calls to action; warm up and follow up with purpose.

## Weaknesses

- Hype wording (cutting-edge, transform, ideal solution) and long, generic bodies full of filler.
- The Praise-Picture-Push template leans on flattery and vague synergy.
- Template 3 labels its own sections (Why This Matters, Next Steps), which reads like a form letter.
- Placeholders are mostly generic and none uses a real observation.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Front-loading the purpose, matching the recipient's tone, and value-oriented asks.
