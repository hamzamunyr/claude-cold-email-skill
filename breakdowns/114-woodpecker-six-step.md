# Woodpecker Six-Step Cold Email Guide Breakdown

**Source:** Woodpecker blog "How to Write a Cold Email: 6 Steps + Templates" (January 2026, updated July 2026), from a cold email platform vendor. A different page from the earlier Woodpecker sources.
**Type:** structure guide with 6 sample emails (6 added to the count)
**Performance data:** It quotes an average reply rate of about 3.4% and calls 5 to 8% strong, from its own earlier statistics post. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SaaS sales | "Idea for scaling {{COMPANY}} outbound" | Hiring several SDRs while expanding to a market; teams at that stage need more outbound; one benefit; does that match your experience? |
| 2 | Agency | "Thought on {{COMPANY}}'s {{CHANNEL}} results" | A specific observation, the usual problem at this stage, a similar client result, offer to send the main changes |
| 3 | Recruiting | "{{ROLE}} opportunity at {{COMPANY}}" | Their specific project stood out, the role and focus, a short conversation about the role |
| 4 | Referral | "{{MUTUAL_CONTACT}} suggested I reach out" | A named contact mentioned their initiative, a similar company result, a short conversation or the right person on the team |
| 5 | Partnership | "{{COMPANY}} and {{YOUR_COMPANY}}" | Both serve the same audience on different parts of a process, a partnership idea, compare where offers overlap |
| 6 | Follow-up | same thread, empty subject | One new detail relevant to the earlier message, why it matters, is this still a priority, or should I close the loop? |

## Cadence and timing

Follow up two to three times in the same thread, each with a new reason to respond (a customer result, a clearer problem explanation, a different benefit, a resource, an objection answer, a new signal or a simpler ask); stop automation once they reply.

## Weaknesses

- The first template is a product pitch for the vendor's own tool.
- The recruiting and partnership templates need a genuinely specific detail or they fall flat.
- Almost every ask is the same shape (a question), so test other asks.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Six steps: edit the From line, a subject that earns the open, an intro about the recipient, one benefit tied to the problem, one easy call to action, a simple signature; 50 to 100 words; use AI to research signals, not to write lazily. The follow-up that asks whether it is still a priority or whether to close the loop.
