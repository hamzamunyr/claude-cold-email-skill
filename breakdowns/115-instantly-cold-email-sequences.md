# Instantly Cold Email Sequences Breakdown

**Source:** Instantly blog "Cold Email Sequence Templates" (January 2026, updated October 2026), from a cold email platform vendor. A different page from the earlier Instantly sources.
**Type:** structure guide with 15 sample emails (15 added to the count)
**Performance data:** Not provided. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Lead generation sequence | "{{Personalized Reason or Quick Benefit}} for {{Prospect's Company}}", "Quick idea for {{Goal}}", "Worth a quick chat?" | An observation and a quick idea, a case study or article from a similar company, a 15 minute direct ask |
| 4 to 6 | Referral sequence | "{{Mutual Contact}} suggested I reach out", "Thought this might help with {{Relevant Challenge}}", "Open to a call this month?" | A name drop intro, a helpful resource, a quick check-in |
| 7 to 9 | Meeting request sequence | "Quick call about {{Specific Topic}}?", "How {{Similar Company}} approached {{Relevant Topic}}", "Should I close the file?" | A direct ask tied to a goal, proof or perspective, a last ping |
| 10 to 12 | Non-responder sequence | "Wanted to circle back, {{First Name}}", "Quick tip for {{their role or company}}", "Should I take this off my list?" | A light nudge with a case study, an alternative tip, permission to close out |
| 13 to 15 | Interested but quiet | "Still interested in connecting?", "Resource for {{topic you discussed}}", "Wanted to close the loop" | A meeting reminder with a scheduling link, a case study or demo video, a no-rush door-open close |

## Cadence and timing

Three to four emails across roughly two weeks: day 1 intro, day 3 to 4 add useful context or proof, day 6 to 8 a different angle, day 10 to 14 a simple close. Use a different sequence for new leads, referrals, meeting requests, non-responders and warm prospects who went quiet; give every follow-up a fresh reason; stop when they say yes or no.

## Weaknesses

- Several subjects are generic or guilt-flavored (circle back, take this off my list, close the file).
- Bodies lean on just checking in style in the non-responder set, which the page itself says to avoid.
- The cold email 1 hooks on a placeholder observation; with no real one it is empty.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Separate sequences by situation, with a clear job per touch and stop rules.
