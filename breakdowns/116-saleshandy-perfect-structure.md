# Saleshandy Perfect Cold Email Structure Breakdown

**Source:** Saleshandy blog "What is a Perfect Cold Email Structure to Get 10X Replies?" (2024, updated 2026), from a cold email software vendor. A different page from the earlier Saleshandy sources.
**Type:** structure guide with 5 sample emails (5 added to the count)
**Performance data:** The page claims 70% open and 50%+ reply rates averages from its sequences, a 45% reply rate for a concise email versus 2% for a long one, and over 60% of meetings from humor. These are not credible against first-party data elsewhere in this repo, and the sample emails are shown as images that I could not view directly; the text below comes from an extraction of the image text. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generic example (the bad one) | "Proposal for {{Company_Name}}" | Hope you are well, we are the best lead generation provider, helped 100+ companies, a call this week |
| 2 | Concise example (the good one) | "Quick question regarding {{Company_Name}}'s lead gen" | Noticed they are hiring SDRs, ramping takes weeks, a pool of vetted candidates, show 3 candidates? |
| 3 | Humor opener | not shown | I promise this is not another SEO pitch, are you happy with your pipeline |
| 4 | GIF follow-up | not shown | Bumping this up in case it got buried, a GIF, let me know your thoughts |
| 5 | Value-first call to action | not shown | Are you facing lead gen issues; offer a teardown video of their landing page with 3 fixes |

## Cadence and timing

Not a cadence guide. Five parts: an opening subject and pre-header, a hyper-personalized intro, 100 to 125 words, a value-giving call to action, and a simple signature with at most one link.

## Weaknesses

- The humor and GIF advice conflicts with other sources here, and the GIF follow-up is a bump email.
- The headline results are not credible.
- Image-only examples I could not verify.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The generic versus concise comparison (same offer, one specific observation and a small ask), and the teardown-video offer as value before a call.
