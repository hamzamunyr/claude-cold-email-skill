## What is genuinely useful here

- A five-part format: subject (short, understated, like a colleague), opening line (about the prospect, never my name is...), value (two or three lines on the outcome), one low-friction ask (a single question answerable in one line, no menu of options), a simple human signature. Under about 120 words; anything that needs scrolling on a phone is too long.
