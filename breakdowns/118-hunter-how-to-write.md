# Hunter How to Write a Cold Email Breakdown

**Source:** Hunter blog "How to Write a Cold Email That Converts (Without Being Pushy)" (2022, updated 2026), from an email finder vendor. A different page from the earlier Hunter sources.
**Type:** structure and practice guide (no sample emails, no email count added)
**Performance data:** It cites a 19.6% response rate for the author's own link-building email and an old study of a 5.81% higher click rate for 95 words versus 170 words. The sample emails are screenshots I did not view, so I count none. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## Cadence and timing

Not a cadence guide.

## Weaknesses

- Old advice; the examples are images that are not transcribed.

## What is genuinely useful here

- Nine tips: a personal short subject, outline the single goal first, a catchy opening (a question, a mutual connection, a genuine compliment, their problem, or a trigger), personalize (small batches by hand, large ones with data), keep it around 95 words, provide value not a pitch, one precise call to action, write like a human in plain text, and keep it scannable with 1 to 2 sentence paragraphs; drop I hope you are doing well.
