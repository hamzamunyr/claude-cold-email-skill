# Zendesk Cold Email Templates Breakdown

**Source:** Zendesk blog "7 cold email templates that skyrocket response rates" (April 2026), from a customer service software vendor
**Type:** structure guide with 7 sample emails (7 added to the count)
**Performance data:** It quotes one outdated figure that personalized emails have about a 10% higher open rate. I read the page through an extraction summary and could not verify the figures, so treat them as unverified vendor claims.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quick question (right person) | not provided | Asks who handles decisions in a department and how to reach them |
| 2 | Third-party connection | not provided | Found their name on a source, has a solution for an industry pain, asks who the right person is |
| 3 | PAS | not provided | Notices negative customer service reviews, agitates, product fixes it, would you like to hear more? |
| 4 | AIDA | not provided | What if a product could solve X, a customer result with a percent, a call this week |
| 5 | Straight to business | not provided | Is your support system losing you money, a percent cost drop for large companies, a call this week |
| 6 | Paint a picture | not provided | Losing a candidate because you cannot find their record, imagine one organized place, a quick demo |
| 7 | Something useful | not provided | Saw a post, a genuine reaction, the takeaways, did you see this related article |

## Cadence and timing

Not a cadence guide. The page names structured formulas: quick question, third-party connection, PAS, AIDA, straight to business, paint a picture, something useful.

## Weaknesses

- No subject lines at all.
- Template 3 points to negative reviews as a hook, which can feel invasive, and 4 and 5 bake in invented percentage results.
- Template 1 opens with a stiff intro and a formal greeting.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The two right-person templates (1 and 2) as simple routing asks, and the something-useful template that engages with their own post.
