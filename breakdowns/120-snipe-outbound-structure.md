# Snipe Outbound Cold Email Structure Breakdown

**Source:** Snipe Outbound blog "Cold Email Structure: How to Write B2B Emails + Brief", from an outbound agency
**Type:** structure guide with 1 sample email (1 added to the count)
**Performance data:** The page contains no statistics. It describes a research-to-copy workflow, so there are no claims to verify.

## The emails

| # | Template | Subject line | Core idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Five-line annotated example | "[specific initiative]" | A reviewed signal with its source date, why it can create a consequence, what peers get and how, an approved customer result with context, one comparison question |

## Cadence and timing

Not a cadence guide; it says to build sequences without just-checking-in, add a legitimate angle to each, and define stop conditions, suppression, ownership and opt-out handling before launch.

## Weaknesses

- The page is a process document with little copy, and its placeholders are bracketed fields.

## What is genuinely useful here

- The most careful structure in the batch: five parts (relevant opener, reason framed as a hypothesis, value with a mechanism, proof that matches an approved record, one smallest-decision ask). Practices: match research depth to account value, do not disguise a scraped token as research, state the reason with hedged words like often, can or may, keep each proof point exactly as approved (customer, result, time window, conditions), read the message aloud, change one variable at a time, and report the funnel in stages with bounced, unsubscribed, out-of-office and wrong-person outcomes kept separate.
