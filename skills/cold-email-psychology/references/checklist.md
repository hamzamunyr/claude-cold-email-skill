# Pre-Send Checklist

Run every email through this before delivering it. Fix any line that fails.

- [ ] First line is about the prospect, not the sender
- [ ] The opening shows a real, verifiable observation, not a generic compliment
- [ ] Under 120 words
- [ ] One clear CTA, and it is low friction (no meeting ask unless value is obvious)
- [ ] Includes a cost-of-inaction line, phrased as a question, not an accusation
- [ ] Any proof is real. No fake names, stats, or results
- [ ] One concrete claim a competitor could not make
- [ ] Plain, conversational words. No jargon or hype
- [ ] No em dashes
- [ ] 2 to 3 subject line options provided
