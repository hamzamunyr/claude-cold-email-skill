# Calls to Action

Emails analyzed so far: 21 (Clay, Apollo, Salesloft).

| CTA type | Example shape | When it works | Count | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interest check | "If I could help with [problem], would that be useful?" or "Could this work for [company]?" | Cold first touch. Best default | 5 | Clay, Apollo, Salesloft |
| Short asset offer | "Can I send a short video, breakdown or case study showing how?" | You have something quick to show. Lowest friction | 4 | Clay, Apollo |
| Short call ask (time-boxed) | "Can we do a 10 minute call?" | Value is clear and the prospect is senior. Name the duration | 4 | Apollo, Salesloft |
| Specific time options | "Do you have time Tuesday or Wednesday?" | Warm leads or strong proof. Two named options beat "when are you free?" | 2 | Salesloft |
| Show-me offer | "Want me to walk you through it?" | You have a clear demo of the idea you just described | 2 | Clay |
| Open offer of help | "Let me know if there is anything else I can help with" | Resource shares and warm follow-ups. Low pressure, low reply rate | 2 | Salesloft |
| Opinion ask | "I'd love to know what you think of it" | You shared an article or insight. Easy to answer in one line | 1 | Salesloft |
| No-oriented question | Ask if they would be against hearing how peers do it | Skeptical or senior buyers. Keep the wording simple | 1 | Salesloft |
| Invite offer | "Want an invite to the session?" | Free event or session that educates first | 1 | Clay |
| Referral ask (breakup) | "Is there someone else I should speak to?" | Final follow-up, or when the contact may be wrong | 1 | Clay |
| Calendar link | A link plus two specific time blocks | Only after the prospect shows interest | 1 (conditional) | Apollo |
| Phone number | Give a phone number to call if interested | Weak in cold email. Adds an extra step | 1 | Salesloft |
| Direct meeting ask | "Do you have 15 minutes this week?" | Only when value is already obvious. Raises resistance in a cold first email | 2 (Clay advises against, Salesloft uses one) | Clay, Salesloft |

Rule: one CTA per email. Phrase it as a small offer, not a request for their time. Clay advises against asking for a call in the first email, while Apollo and Salesloft use short call asks in several templates. Default to a smaller ask, and use a call ask for senior prospects when the proof is strong. Do not stack a link, an opinion ask and a phone number in one email.
