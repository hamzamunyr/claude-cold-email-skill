# Example Emails

All examples are original. Bracketed items are placeholders to fill with real facts. Never invent proof.

---

## Example 1: Returns software for an ecommerce brand

**Prospect:** Head of Customer Experience at a Shopify fashion brand
**Psychology:** specificity (job post trigger), loss aversion, social proof, commitment minimization
**Pattern from:** Clay (#001)

**Subject options**
1. returns at [Brand]
2. your support hire
3. quick question on returns

**Email**

Hi [First name],

Your open support role lists processing returns by hand as a main duty, so I'm guessing returns eat a big chunk of your team's week.

We automate return requests, labels and refund checks, so agents only handle the odd cases.

When peak season hits, how will you keep returns from pulling agents off live chat?

[Similar brand] [real result] in [timeframe] after switching.

Would a 2 minute walkthrough be useful?

[Your name]

**Why it works:** the first line proves research, the question shows a cost they may not have priced in, the proof is a similar company, and the ask is tiny.
