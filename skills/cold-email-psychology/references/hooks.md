# Hooks and Subject Lines

Templates analyzed so far: 1.

## Opening line patterns

| Pattern | Formula | Count | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trigger event plus insight | "I noticed [event]. In my experience that usually means [insight]." | 1 | Clay |
| Job posting hook | Reference a duty in their open role that points at a problem you solve | 1 | Clay |
| Headcount growth hook | Mention a visible team expansion and ask how they will handle the next step | 1 | Clay |
| Metric change hook | Cite a public data point that moved (for example web traffic) | 1 | Clay |
| New role hook | Congratulate on a new role, then tie it to a hiring signal | 1 | Clay |

Rule: the first line is about the prospect, not you.
